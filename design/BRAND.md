# Zane Brand Kit · proposal

**Approval:** No ecosystem-wide preferences are confirmed by this document. Preserve the visual identity and instructions of the site you are editing.

| Site | Observed in the source |
| --- | --- |
| Personal | Warm off-white, near-black text, indigo links, quiet rules, a responsive photo-led project directory. |
| Cool Strangers Club / BuildFest | The Club homepage uses EB Garamond and an invitation-led voice. BuildFest uses its own event photography, graphics and assets. |
| Photo Notes | Soft blue, touches of gold, Caveat lettering and an illustrated photograph. The opening copy speaks directly to the recipient. |
| ACL | Warm neutral UI, system type, compact controls and a denser schedule view that serves the festival task. |
| Coachella | Shares the practical scheduler styling pattern with ACL and uses its own festival data, imagery and destinations. |

**Proposed direction.** Make the subject lead: photographs on a portfolio, invitations on Club, the personal note on Photo Notes, and the schedule on a festival app. Start with warm neutral surfaces and a single emphasis color where that fits. Use regular reading sizes and fluid spacing; keep dense tools efficient. Studio trials indigo, a serif headline and system type in the form. Its tokens are documented in [design-tokens.json](design-tokens.json) for reference.

**Decisions for Zane.** Confirm whether the warm palette and serif display style should extend beyond Studio; choose any preferred fonts, image crops and motion style; identify examples of copy that feel most like you. Until then, avoid migrating colors, typography, motion or copy across existing projects.

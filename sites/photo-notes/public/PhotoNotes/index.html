Zane’s photo notes

This photo is yours.

You’re the only person that will ever be given this photo. I wrote a note on the back just for you, and I keep a second copy with your name on it as a reminder of who it belongs to.

About this project

I love taking photos, but I never really knew what to do with them. Posting on Instagram felt unfulfilling—a photo gets a few seconds of attention, then disappears into the feed.

I choose to give them as gifts instead. I’d rather have a photo cherished by one person than scrolled past by fifty.

Now that you’ve been given this photo, it belongs to you (and only you) forever.

I hope you enjoy it.— Zane

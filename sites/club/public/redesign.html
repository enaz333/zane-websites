Small print, minus the small print.

What kind of plans?

Sometimes loud, sometimes laid-back. Think dinner, a show, or something active. Each gathering brings together a group of people around something to do.

There’s no fixed schedule. We’ll email you when we have a good fit.

What does it cost?

Joining is free. Some plans have a cost, like your dinner or a show ticket. We’ll always tell you upfront, and you never have to spend money to be part of the club.

Who’s behind this?

A group of friends who grew up in Austin and wanted to make meeting new people easier. We run this as volunteers, just for fun.

Say hi to Zane on Instagram ↗ or send us an email.

What’s the vibe?

Show up with intention. Include the person you don’t know. Be kind, and respect people’s boundaries—we have zero tolerance for making people uncomfortable.

Once you’re here, keep hanging out, host something, or get your own invite cards. Have an idea or a concern? We’re all ears.

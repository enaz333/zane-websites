01

Quick start guide

Follow these three steps in order. Keep this page open as you go.

  1. 1

    Install Scenery & sign in

    Download Scenery on your Mac or iPad. Open the app, then create an account or sign in with Apple or email and password.

    Use that same account in the next step.

    Download Scenery ↗
  2. 2

    Unlock your free month of Pro

    1. Open the account dashboard and sign in with your Scenery account.
    2. Find Redeem a promo code, paste the code below, and click Redeem.
    USC-SOCIAL-IMPACT-26

    One month of Pro free. Redeem before the end of the month.

    Open account dashboard ↗
  3. 3

    Set up your ChatGPT building helper

    BuildFest Support gives ChatGPT the Scenery reference guides so it can walk you through building and troubleshooting. You only need to install it once.

    1. Download the plugin file

      Save BuildFest_Support_Plugin.zip to your computer. It will usually appear in your Downloads folder. Keep it zipped.

      Download plugin ZIP ↓

    2. Open ChatGPT’s Plugins page

      Click the button and sign in to ChatGPT. The Plugins page opens in a new tab, so you can keep following this guide.

      Open ChatGPT Plugins ↗

    3. Upload the ZIP you downloaded

      Where uploads are enabled, choose Add → Upload plugin (sometimes labeled Upload plugin archive). Select BuildFest_Support_Plugin.zip from Downloads and follow the prompts. If the plugin shows an install or + button, click it.

      Don’t see Add or Upload? Your account or workspace may not allow plugin uploads. Ask a BuildFest mentor to help you get access.

    4. Start a chat with BuildFest Support

      Start a new chat. Type @ in the message box, search for BuildFest Support, and click its name in the menu. Then send a question like this:

      I’m new to Scenery. Help me build my first AR experience, one step at a time. Start by asking what I want to make.

      You’re ready when BuildFest Support is selected in your message and ChatGPT replies to your question.

04

Useful resources

Share your experience and find ready-made assets for your build.

SHARE YOUR EXPERIENCE

Make a QR code

Use TinyQR to turn your Scenery share link into a QR code. Free, with no account required.

  1. Paste your experience’s share link.
  2. Download the code as a PNG.
  3. Scan it on another phone to test it.
Create a free QR code ↗

Static codes don’t expire. Keep the experience link available.

3D MODELS · USDZ

Find a 3D object

Scenery imports 3D models only in .usdz format. Other model formats must be converted first.

CGTrader · USDZ models ↗A large library of free and paid 3D models. Check that USDZ is included before downloading.Sketchfab ↗Browse downloadable models and choose USDZ when offered. A free account is required.

Check the model’s license and any required credit before using it.

2D GRAPHICS & SOUND EFFECTS

Find graphics & sound

Use PNG/JPG for images and MP3, WAV or M4A for sound.

Kenney ↗Free CC0 game assets, including icons, UI and sprites. Pick 2D packs and use the PNG files inside.Pixabay Sound Effects ↗Free sound effects and ambience. Preview a sound, then download the MP3 to add to Scenery.
Bring your assets into Scenery

Unzip downloaded asset packs first. In Scenery, open Assets → Upload asset, or drag your USDZ, PNG, JPG, MP3, WAV or M4A file into the editor. Other 3D formats need conversion to USDZ.
